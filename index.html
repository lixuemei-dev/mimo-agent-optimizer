Optimization Strategies

Choose the right strategy for your use case

🎯

Token Budget

Trim context windows, compress prompts, and deduplicate tool results. Targets 30–50% token reduction with minimal quality loss.

⚡

Latency Tuning

Enable streaming, parallel tool dispatch, and reduce max tokens. Focuses on time-to-first-token and total completion time.

💰

Cost Control

Model tiering, response caching, and per-session budget caps. Keeps monthly costs within defined thresholds.
